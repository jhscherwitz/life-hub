Get started in three steps

About two minutes, start to finish.

  1. Download and open it

    Get the installer for Windows or Mac. If your computer warns you about it, see the notes below.

  2. Sign in with Google

    Life Hub asks to see your calendar and email so it can show them to you. You can sign out any time.

  3. Add a free AI key

    Get a free Gemini key from Google AI Studio and paste it into Life Hub. It costs nothing.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That's Windows SmartScreen. It shows for new apps that aren't widely downloaded yet.

  1. Click More info.
  2. Click Run anyway.
Mac says Life Hub "can't be opened"

Life Hub isn't from the Mac App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time.

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Life Hub.

Your data stays yours

Life Hub runs on your own computer. There's no Life Hub server and no account.

  • It reads your calendar and email to show them to you, on your computer.
  • It can save email drafts for you to check, but it never sends email for you.
  • Your keys and saved passwords are encrypted on your computer.
  • The AI uses your own free Gemini key, between you and Google.
  • The code is open, so anyone can check what it does.
