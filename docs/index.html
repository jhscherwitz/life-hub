A project by Jacob Scherwitz

Life Hub

A personal desktop dashboard that runs my day: calendar, email, tasks, Canvas grades, news and music on one page, with an AI assistant that doesn't just answer questions but does things for me.

See the code on GitHub How it's built Electron · React · TypeScript · Gemini

What it does

Built for my own routine as a student, and connected to everything I use.

Google Calendar

Your day at a glance

A timeline of today, what's coming up next, countdowns, and when to leave so you're not late.

Gmail

Only the email that matters

See who's waiting on a reply, get your inbox sorted into look into, archive and probably delete, and have replies written for you as drafts.

Free Gemini AI

An assistant that does things

Ask it to add tasks and events, write an email to your professor, look something up, check your week or rearrange your widgets, and it does it.

Canvas

Grades and due dates

Your grade in every class and the assignments coming up, right next to the rest of your day.

Spotify and more

Music in the sidebar

See and control what's playing in Spotify, YouTube or any other app on Windows, or play free radio.

Built-in browser

Browse without leaving

Tabs, an ad and tracker blocker, find in page, saved passwords and your Chrome bookmarks. The AI can read the page with you.

Morning briefing

Wake up to a plan

A short summary of your day each morning: what's on, what's due, the weather and how long your drive will take.

Widgets

Make it yours

Weather, news, stocks, scores, commute, groceries, daily tasks, notes and more, in the sizes and order you want.

How it's built

About 23,000 lines of TypeScript, with 300+ unit tests.

AI agent

A tool-use loop

Gemini function calling with 25+ tools, streamed answers, a Stop button that cancels mid-step, and automatic fallback between models when the free allowance runs out.

Electron

Secure by design

The page talks to the app only through a typed bridge, with no Node in the page. Secrets are encrypted with the OS keychain, and browser tabs run sandboxed.

Sync

Two-way Google Tasks

Remembers how each task looked at the last sync, so whichever side changed wins and deletes carry across, without timestamps from one side.

Undo

Everything is reversible

Every action the AI takes returns an undo token: events move back, cancelled ones come back, and the old dashboard layout is restored.

Private by design

Life Hub runs on my own computer. There's no server and no account.

  • It reads my calendar and email to show them to me, on my computer.
  • It can save email drafts for me to check, but it never sends email itself.
  • Keys and saved passwords are encrypted with the OS keychain.
  • The AI uses my own free Gemini key, directly with Google.
  • The code is open, so anyone can check what it does.
