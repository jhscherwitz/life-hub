Privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Life Hub is a desktop app for Mac and Windows. It runs entirely on your own computer. The person who makes Life Hub has no server, does not run any accounts, and never receives your data.

What Life Hub accesses

When you sign in with Google, Life Hub asks for permission to:

  • See your Google Calendar events, to show your schedule, your next meeting and when to leave.
  • Read your Gmail messages, to show the emails that need a reply.
  • Create Gmail drafts, so that when you click Draft reply, the reply is saved in your Gmail Drafts folder. Life Hub never sends email; you review and send drafts yourself.

Where your data goes

  • Your computer. Your Google sign-in and any API key you enter are stored encrypted with your operating system's keychain. Tasks, notes and settings are saved in Life Hub's app data folder on your computer.
  • Google. Life Hub talks directly to Google's Calendar and Gmail APIs from your computer.
  • Anthropic, only if you turn it on. If you paste your own Anthropic API key into Settings, Life Hub sends your calendar, task titles and the emails it is working on to Anthropic so Claude can write your briefing, decide which emails need a reply and draft replies. This is governed by your own agreement with Anthropic. Without a key, nothing is sent to Anthropic.
  • Weather. The place you choose is sent to Open-Meteo for the weather.
  • Updates. Life Hub checks GitHub for new versions. This shares nothing about you beyond what any download does.

Life Hub does not sell your data, show ads, use your data for anything other than showing it to you, or use it to train AI models.

Google user data

Life Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Click Sign out in Life Hub's Settings to delete your Google sign-in from your computer and remove Life Hub's access to your account. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling Life Hub and deleting its app data folder removes everything else.
